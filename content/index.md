## Vault Structure

```text
Home
├── 01_References          → books, papers, source notes
├── 02_Core_Concepts       → MOCs (hubs)
├── 03-Glossary            → short definitions
├── 04_Figures_and_Media   → diagrams, timelines, Excalidraw
├── 05_Templates           → note templates
└── 09-Microbiology
    ├── Fundamentals/          → concept notes
    ├── Microbiology History/
    ├── Lab/                   → method notes
    ├── Organisms/             → pathogen pages
    ├── Diseases by System/    → CNS, lung, UTI, SSTI…
    ├── Antimicrobials and AMR/ → drugs, mechanisms, stewardship
    ├── Public Health/         → epidemiology, IPC, vaccines, One Health
    └── AI and Bioinformatics/ → computational notes
```

Plus, at the root: `Microbiology Map.canvas` (visual index) and `Encyclopedia.base` (database views).
