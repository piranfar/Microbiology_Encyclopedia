# Paul Ehrlich

**1854–1915 · Germany** · Founder of chemotherapy / “magic bullet” concept.

## Contribution

In 1909, developed Salvarsan (arsphenamine), the first effective medicinal treatment for syphilis (spirochetes). Introduced selective toxicity — drugs that hit the pathogen harder than the host.

## Why It Still Matters

Every antimicrobial and many targeted therapies inherit the “magic bullet” idea. Bridges microscopy/staining chemistry to drug discovery.
