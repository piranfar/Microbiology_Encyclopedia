# Jennifer Doudna

**b. 1964 · USA** · Co-developer of CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing.

## Contribution

With [[Emmanuelle Charpentier]] (2012), adapted the bacterial CRISPR-Cas antiviral immune system into a programmable genome-editing tool — rewriting how genes can be studied and engineered.

## Why It Still Matters

CRISPR is central to microbial genetics research, gene-drive debates, therapeutics, and tools for studying virulence and resistance genes.
