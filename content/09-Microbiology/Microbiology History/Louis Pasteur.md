# Louis Pasteur

**1822–1895 · France** · Architect of germ theory and vaccines.

## Contribution

- Disproved spontaneous generation (swan-neck flask experiments).
- Established microbial causes of fermentation and disease ([[Germ Theory]]).
- Invented pasteurization.
- Developed vaccines (notably anthrax and rabies) using attenuation ideas.

## Why It Still Matters

Pasteur linked laboratory microbiology to public health and immunization. Pasteurization and vaccine logic remain everyday tools.
