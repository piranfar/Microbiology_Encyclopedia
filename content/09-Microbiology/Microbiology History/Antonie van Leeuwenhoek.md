# Antonie van Leeuwenhoek

**c. 1632–1723 · Delft** · Often called the “Father of Microbiology.”

## Contribution

Built high-quality single-lens microscopes and was the first to observe and describe single-celled organisms (“animalcules”) — bacteria and protozoa — in letters to the Royal Society (1670s).

## Why It Still Matters

Microbiology begins with *seeing*. Direct observation made microbes real objects of science and opened the path to germ theory centuries later.
