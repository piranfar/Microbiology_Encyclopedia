# Edward Jenner

**1749–1823 · England** · Pioneer of vaccination.

## Contribution

In 1796, demonstrated that inoculation with cowpox material could protect against smallpox — the first successful vaccine in the modern sense — founding practical immunization.

## Why It Still Matters

Vaccination remains one of medicine’s highest-impact interventions. Jenner’s logic (related, milder agent → immunity to a deadly one) underpins live-attenuated and many later vaccine strategies.
