# Ignaz Semmelweis

**1818–1865 · Vienna** · Pioneer of infection control.

## Contribution

In the 1840s, showed that handwashing with chlorinated lime in obstetrical clinics drastically cut puerperal (“childbed”) fever — arguing that “cadaverous particles” transmitted on physicians’ hands caused disease.

## Why It Still Matters

Hand hygiene is still the cheapest, highest-yield infection-control intervention. Semmelweis anticipated germ theory before microbes were accepted as the cause.
