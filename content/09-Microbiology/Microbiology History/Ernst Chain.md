# Ernst Chain

**1906–1979 · Germany / UK** · Biochemist of the penicillin project.

## Contribution

Key member of the Oxford penicillin team with [[Howard Florey]]: isolation, chemical characterization, and development of penicillin as a therapeutic agent (1940s).

## Why It Still Matters

Highlights that antibiotics are chemistry + biology + manufacturing — not only a Petri-dish story. Nobel Prize 1945 with Fleming and Florey.
