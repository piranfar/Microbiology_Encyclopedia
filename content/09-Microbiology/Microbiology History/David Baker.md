# David Baker

**b. 1962 · USA** · Pioneer of computational protein design.

## Contribution

Advanced tools (e.g. RoseTTAFold and design pipelines) to not only predict structures but **design new proteins** that do not exist in nature — enzymes, binders, and materials with programmable functions.

## Why It Still Matters

Opens routes to synthetic biosensors, novel antivirals, custom binding proteins, and potentially new antibiotic modalities — design, not only discovery.
