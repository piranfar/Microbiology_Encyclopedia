# Robert Koch

**1843–1910 · Germany** · Founder of medical bacteriology.

## Contribution

- Identified causative agents of anthrax, tuberculosis, and cholera.
- Developed rigorous culture methods and staining approaches.
- Formulated [[Koch’s Postulates]] — criteria linking a microbe to a disease.
- Solidified [[Germ Theory]] with specific etiologic proofs.

## Why It Still Matters

Koch’s logic still structures how we think about etiology, culture, and pathogen discovery — even when molecular methods replace pure culture.
