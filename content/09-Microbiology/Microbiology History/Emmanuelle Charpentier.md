# Emmanuelle Charpentier

**b. 1968 · France** · Co-developer of CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing.

## Contribution

With [[Jennifer Doudna]] (2012), showed how CRISPR-Cas9 can be programmed to cut specific DNA sequences, launching modern genome editing from a bacterial immune mechanism.

## Why It Still Matters

Same as Doudna: a microbial defense system became a universal genetic tool — and a reminder that basic bacteriology yields transformative technology.
