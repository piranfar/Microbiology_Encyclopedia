# Carl Woese

**1928–2012 · USA** · Redrew the tree of life.

## Contribution

In 1977, used 16S ribosomal RNA phylogenetics to discover Archaea as a third domain of life alongside Bacteria and Eukarya — replacing a simpler prokaryote/eukaryote split.

## Why It Still Matters

16S (and related marker genes) still power microbial identification, microbiome surveys, and phylogenetic diagnostics. Taxonomy became molecular.
