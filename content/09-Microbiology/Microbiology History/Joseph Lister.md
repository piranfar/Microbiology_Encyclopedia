# Joseph Lister

**1827–1912 · Britain** · Founder of antiseptic surgery.

## Contribution

From 1867, applied Pasteur’s germ ideas to surgery: carbolic acid (phenol) to sterilize instruments and wounds, founding antiseptic (later aseptic) surgical practice.

## Why It Still Matters

Modern sterile technique, skin prep, and OR discipline descend from Lister’s insight that surgical infection is preventable microbial contamination.
