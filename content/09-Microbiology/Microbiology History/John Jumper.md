# John Jumper

**b. 1985 · USA** · Lead author / key developer of AlphaFold2.

## Contribution

Central scientific lead on **AlphaFold** with [[Demis Hassabis]] at DeepMind: high-accuracy protein structure prediction from sequence, transforming structural biology workflows.

## Why It Still Matters

Same applied angle as Hassabis: microbial proteins become structurally explorable at scale — useful for understanding pathogens and designing interventions.
