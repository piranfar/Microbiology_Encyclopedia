# Demis Hassabis

**b. 1976 · UK** · Co-leader of AlphaFold (Google DeepMind).

## Contribution

With [[John Jumper]] and the DeepMind team, developed **AlphaFold**, an AI system that predicts protein 3D structure from amino acid sequence — solving a long-standing grand challenge (breakthrough recognition ~2020–2021; Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2024 with Jumper and [[David Baker]]).

## Why It Still Matters

Microbial virulence factors, viral spike proteins, and drug targets are proteins. Structure prediction accelerates hypothesis generation for antimicrobials, vaccines, and enzyme function.
