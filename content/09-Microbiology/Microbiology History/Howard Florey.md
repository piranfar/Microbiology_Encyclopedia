# Howard Florey

**1898–1968 · Australia / UK** · Turned penicillin into a usable drug.

## Contribution

With [[Ernst Chain]] and the Oxford team (1940s), purified penicillin, demonstrated clinical efficacy, and enabled wartime scale-up — converting Fleming’s observation into therapy.

## Why It Still Matters

Shows the translational pipeline: discovery → chemistry/production → clinical use. Shared Nobel Prize (1945) with Fleming and Chain.
