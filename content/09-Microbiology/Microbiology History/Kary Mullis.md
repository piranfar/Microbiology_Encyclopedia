# Kary Mullis

**1944–2019 · USA** · Inventor of [[PCR]].

## Contribution

In 1983, invented the polymerase chain reaction — enzymatic amplification of specific DNA segments — a bedrock of modern molecular diagnostics and research.

## Why It Still Matters

Clinical virology/bacteriology panels, genotyping, and much of AMR gene detection depend on PCR logic. Extensions include [[Reverse Transcription Polymerase Chain Reaction (RT-PCR)]].
