# Alexander Fleming

**1881–1955 · Scotland** · Discoverer of penicillin.

## Contribution

In 1928, observed that *Penicillium notatum* produced a substance that inhibited staphylococci — penicillin, the first broadly useful antibiotic scaffold of the modern era.

## Why It Still Matters

Opened the antibiotic age. Also a cautionary tale: discovery alone was not enough — purification and scale-up ([[Howard Florey]], [[Ernst Chain]]) made it medicine.
