# Gonorrhea

**System:** [[Genitourinary Infections]] · **Parent MOC:** [[MOC - Diseases by System]]

## One-Sentence Definition

Gonorrhea is a sexually transmitted infection caused by *Neisseria gonorrhoeae*, presenting as urethritis/cervicitis and capable of causing disseminated infection, PID, and neonatal ophthalmia — with rapidly evolving antimicrobial resistance.

## Clinical Presentation
- Urethritis: purulent discharge, dysuria (often symptomatic in men)
- Cervicitis/PID: often asymptomatic until complications
- Pharyngeal/rectal infection: frequently asymptomatic
- Disseminated gonococcal infection (DGI): arthritis-dermatitis syndrome, tenosynovitis
- Neonatal conjunctivitis if untreated maternal infection

## Pathogens
| Organism | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| [[Neisseria gonorrhoeae]] | Gram-negative diplococcus; pilus phase variation |
| Coinfection | [[Chlamydia trachomatis]] common — test/treat per guidelines |

## Pathogenesis
- Mucosal attachment/invasion; evades [[Complement System]] and [[Antibodies and Immunoglobulin Classes]] via surface variation ([[Antigenic Variation]])
- Ascending infection → PID, infertility, ectopic pregnancy risk

## Diagnosis
| Step | Test | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| First-line | NAAT ([[PCR]]) on urine or swab | Cervical/urethral/pharyngeal/rectal as exposed |
| Culture | Selective media + AST | **Required** for susceptibility in many settings |
| Disseminated | Blood/ synovial fluid culture | Low yield but important when positive |

## Treatment Principles
- Ceftriaxone-based regimens per local resistance guidelines + chlamydia coverage when not excluded
- Test-of-cure culture when indicated (pharyngeal failure concerns)
- Partner notification; abstain until treated
- **Never rely on oral fluoroquinolones empirically** — widespread resistance

## Prevention
- Barrier protection; screening high-risk networks
- Neonatal prophylaxis (erythromycin/ other per locale) where applicable

## Differential / Pitfalls
- NGU from chlamydia, Mycoplasma genitalium
- Treating without culture capacity in rising ceftriaxone MIC settings — stewardship + surveillance
