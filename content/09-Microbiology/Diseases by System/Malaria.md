# Malaria

**System:** [[Bloodstream and Sepsis]] · **Parent MOC:** [[MOC - Diseases by System]] · **Domain:** [[MOC - Parasitology]]

## One-Sentence Definition

Malaria is a mosquito-borne febrile illness caused by *Plasmodium* species, diagnosed primarily by blood smear or rapid antigen/PCR tests — *P. falciparum* is the medical emergency because of severe disease and cerebral malaria.

## Clinical Presentation
- Cyclic fever, chills, sweats, headache, myalgia
- Anemia, splenomegaly
- Severe: cerebral malaria, ARDS, renal failure, hypoglycemia, high parasitemia
- Relapsing *P. vivax/ovale* (hypnozoites)

## Pathogens
| Species | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| *P. falciparum* | Most severe; cerebral malaria; chloroquine resistance common |
| *P. vivax* / *P. ovale* | Relapse; Duffy negativity protects vs vivax in some populations |
| *P. malariae* | Nephrotic syndrome association (chronic) |
| *P. knowlesi* | Zoonotic; can be severe |

## Pathogenesis
- Sporozoites → liver → merozoites rupture RBCs → cyclic symptoms
- Cytoadherence (falciparum) → microvascular sequestration

## Diagnosis
| Step | Test | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| First-line | Thick/thin **blood smear** | Species ID + parasitemia % |
| Rapid | Antigen card (HRP2/pLDH) | Faster; know false-positive HRP2 after treatment |
| Molecular | [[PCR]] | Travel/imported cases, low parasitemia |
| Travel | Always ask exposure history | Incubation weeks–months |

## Treatment Principles
- Species and resistance map guide therapy (artemisinin combinations for falciparum in most regions)
- *P. vivax/ovale*: add hypnozoite eradication (e.g., primaquine) after G6PD check
- Severe malaria: IV artesunate — urgent

## Prevention
- Chemoprophylaxis for travelers; bed nets; vector control
- No routine human vaccine widely deployed (RTS,S in selected programs)

## Differential / Pitfalls
- Typhoid, dengue, viral hepatitis, sepsis — travel history key
- Missing smear in febrile returned traveler = critical error
