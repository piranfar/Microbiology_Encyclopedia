# Tuberculosis

**System:** [[Respiratory Infections]] · **Parent MOC:** [[MOC - Diseases by System]]

## One-Sentence Definition

Tuberculosis (TB) is a chronic granulomatous infection caused by *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* complex, transmitted by airborne droplets, with latent infection (LTBI) and active pulmonary or extrapulmonary disease forms.

## Clinical Presentation
- Pulmonary: chronic cough, hemoptysis, weight loss, night sweats, fever
- Extrapulmonary: lymphadenitis, pleural, CNS, bone, disseminated (miliary) in immunosuppression
- LTBI: asymptomatic; positive IGRA/TST without active disease features

## Pathogens
| Form | Organism | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Typical TB | [[Mycobacterium tuberculosis]] | Acid-fast; slow growth |
| Related | *M. bovis*, *M. africanum* | Region/epidemiology dependent |
| Mimics | [[Nontuberculous Mycobacteria]] | Different treatment — do not assume TB |

## Pathogenesis
- Inhalation → alveolar macrophages → granuloma (cell-mediated [[Adaptive Immunity]])
- Latency vs reactivation (HIV, TNF inhibitors, malnutrition)
- Cavitation → high transmissibility

## Diagnosis
| Step | Test | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Screening | IGRA or TST | LTBI programs |
| Active pulmonary | Sputum [[Acid-Fast Stain]] + mycobacterial culture | Gold standard for culture/AST |
| Rapid | NAAT (e.g., Xpert MTB/RIF) | Detects MTBC + rifampin resistance marker |
| Resistance | Culture AST + WGS in reference labs | MDR/XDR/XDR-TB epi |
| Imaging | Chest X-ray/CT | Cavities, miliary pattern |

## Treatment Principles
- Active TB: multi-drug regimen (RIPE backbone) for months — public health supervised
- LTBI: shorter rifamycin-based regimens when indicated
- MDR-TB: individualized per AST/WGS
- Isolation + contact investigation ([[Infection Prevention and Control]])

## Prevention
- BCG (variable efficacy; policy by country)
- Treat LTBI in high-risk contacts
- Ventilation in high-risk settings

## Differential / Pitfalls
- NTM lung disease; fungal; malignancy
- Xpert negative does not exclude paucibacillary TB — repeat/clinical judgment
- Do not treat LTBI and active disease with the same casual approach
