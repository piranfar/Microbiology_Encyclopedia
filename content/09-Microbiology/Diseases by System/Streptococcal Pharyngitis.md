# Streptococcal Pharyngitis

**System:** [[HEENT Infections]] · **Parent MOC:** [[MOC - Diseases by System]]

## One-Sentence Definition

Acute pharyngitis caused by [[Streptococcus pyogenes]] (GAS), important mainly to treat symptoms, limit transmission, and prevent acute rheumatic fever in at-risk populations.

## Clinical Presentation
- Sudden sore throat, fever, tender anterior cervical nodes, tonsillar exudate; scarlatiniform rash in scarlet fever
- Usually **no** cough/rhinorrhea (those favor viral)
- Centor / McIsaac scores triage testing

## Pathogens
| Organism | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| [[Streptococcus pyogenes]] | Lancefield group A |
| Viral (majority of pharyngitis) | EBV, adeno, etc. — [[MOC - Virology]] |
| *Fusobacterium* (Lemierre — rare) | Adolescent septic jugular thrombophlebitis |

## Pathogenesis
- Local invasion + streptococcal pyrogenic exotoxins (scarlet fever)
- Post-infectious: acute rheumatic fever, PSGN — [[MOC - Immunology]]

## Diagnosis
| Step | Test | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| RADT | Rapid antigen | Confirm negatives with culture in children per local practice |
| Throat culture | Gold for GAS | |
| Avoid | Testing viral-looking illness indiscriminately | Stewardship |

## Treatment Principles
- Penicillin / amoxicillin drug of choice (GAS remains susceptible)
- Macrolides only if true penicillin allergy — watch resistance
- Symptom care; school return rules after 12–24h antibiotics

## Prevention
- Treat proven GAS to reduce ARF risk where relevant
- Household hygiene; outbreak management in institutions

## Differential / Pitfalls
- Viral pharyngitis, mono (EBV — rash if ampicillin), peritonsillar abscess, epiglottitis (different age/look)
- Treating everyone with sore throat → resistance + CDI risk ([[Clostridioides difficile Infection]])

## Learning Aids

### Clinical Example
> [!example]
> **Case:** 8yo, fever, exudative tonsils, tender nodes, no cough; RADT positive.
> **Question:** Drug of choice and one nonsuppurative complication you prevent?
> **Answer:** Penicillin/amoxicillin for [[Streptococcus pyogenes]]. Prevents acute rheumatic fever (PSGN not clearly prevented by abx the same way — know the teaching nuance).
