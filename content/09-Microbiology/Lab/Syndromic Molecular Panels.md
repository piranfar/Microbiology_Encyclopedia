# Syndromic Molecular Panels

**Related:** [[Multiplex PCR]] · [[qPCR]] · [[Sample Types and Specimen Quality]] · [[MOC - Clinical Microbiology]]

## 1. Principle
> Syndromic panels are clinically bundled multiplex NAATs that test for the most relevant pathogens of a syndrome (respiratory, GI, CNS, pneumonia, joint) from one specimen with standardized interpretation software.

## 2. Step-by-Step Procedure (conceptual)
1. Collect syndrome-appropriate specimen.
2. Load cartridge / plate on closed platform (sample-to-answer common).
3. Automated extraction + [[Multiplex PCR]] / [[Isothermal NAAT]].
4. Software calls each target; lab verifies flags/invalids.
5. Report with comments on colonization and co-detections.

## 3. Interpretation
- Rule-in useful; rule-out depends on panel coverage and NPV at local prevalence.
- Co-detections need clinical adjudication.
- Resistance genes on panels ≠ full AST.

## 4. Clinical Use Cases
- ED/ICU rapid workups; stewardship (stop anti-MRSA/anti-Pseudomonas when panel and cultures allow).
- Outbreak screens for viral respiratory seasons.
