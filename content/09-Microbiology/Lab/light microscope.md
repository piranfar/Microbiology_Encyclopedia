# [[light microscope]] (or optical microscope)

## 1. Principle
> *A **light microscope** (or optical microscope) ==uses visible light and a system of glass lenses to magnify small, often microscopic specimens that are otherwise invisible to the naked eye==. It is a foundational tool in biology, medicine, and education
## 2. Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Preparation:** Place the prepared slide on the stage and secure it with the stage clips. Ensure the specimen is centered over the light source.
2. **Initial Focus:** Rotate the nosepiece to click the lowest power objective lens (typically $4\times$ or $10\times$) into place. Raise the stage fully using the **coarse adjustment knob**, then slowly lower it until the specimen comes into rough focus.
3. **Fine-Tuning:** Use the **fine adjustment knob** to sharpen the image clarity. Adjust the iris diaphragm and condenser to optimize light intensity and contrast.
4. **Magnification Progression:** Switch to higher power objectives ($40\times$). _Only_ use the fine adjustment knob from this point forward to avoid crushing the slide.
5. **Oil Immersion ($100\times$):** If using the $100\times$ lens, add a single drop of immersion oil directly onto the slide cover slip. Click the oil immersion lens into place. The oil prevents light refraction, channeling more light into the lens to maintain high resolution.
