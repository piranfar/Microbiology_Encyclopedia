# Oxygen Requirements in Bacteria

## One-Sentence Definition

Oxygen requirements classify bacteria by their need for O₂ and tolerance of reactive oxygen species — obligate aerobes, facultative anaerobes, obligate anaerobes, aerotolerant anaerobes, and microaerophiles — guiding culture conditions and infection sites.

## Simple Explanation

Some bacteria need air, some die from it, and some prefer a little oxygen — labs culture them differently.

## Detailed Scientific Explanation

| Class | O₂ need | Enzymes (concept) | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Obligate aerobe** | Requires O₂ | Catalase/superoxide dismutase | [[Pseudomonas aeruginosa]], [[Mycobacterium tuberculosis]] |
| **Facultative anaerobe** | O₂ preferred, can ferment | Usually catalase+ | [[Escherichia coli]], [[Staphylococcus aureus]] |
| **Obligate anaerobe** | O₂ toxic | Lack/deficient ROS defenses | *Bacteroides*, *Clostridium*, *Cutibacterium* |
| **Aerotolerant anaerobe** | Ignores O₂; ferments | No respiration with O₂ | *Enterococcus*, *Streptococcus* (many) |
| **Microaerophile** | Low O₂ optimal | Sensitive to atmospheric O₂ | [[Campylobacter jejuni]], [[Helicobacter pylori]] |

**Culture implications**
- Anaerobic blood culture bottles + anaerobic incubation
- Campylobacter 42 °C microaerophilic atmosphere
- TB needs aerobic (slow) culture

**Infection sites**
- Abscess core, devitalized tissue → anaerobes
- Open wounds, lungs → aerobes/facultatives

## Mechanism
O₂ generates superoxide/peroxide; catalase/peroxidase/SOD detoxify — missing enzymes → anaerobiosis requirement.

## Clinical Importance
- Polymicrobial abscess: aerobes + anaerobes together
- Failure to culture anaerobes → incomplete ID

## Diagnostic Relevance
- Guides [[Culture and Isolation]] setup and bottle selection
