# Borrelia burgdorferi

## One-Sentence Definition

*Borrelia burgdorferi* sensu lato spirochetes are tick-transmitted agents of Lyme disease in North America/Europe — causing early localized, disseminated, and late musculoskeletal/neurologic/cardiac manifestations.

## Taxonomy & Morphology
- Spirochaetaceae; spiral bacteria; microaerophilic
- Related: *B. recurrentis* (relapsing fever), [[Treponema pallidum]]

## Virulence Highlights
- Migration through skin and tissues
- Immune evasion (variable surface proteins)
- Tick (*Ixodes*) vector — coinfection with other tick agents possible (ehrlichia, babesia — context)

## Clinical Syndromes
- Erythema migrans (pathognomonic early)
- Disseminated: facial palsy, meningitis, carditis, migratory arthralgias
- Late Lyme arthritis (US pattern)

## Diagnosis
- **Clinical EM** often sufficient in endemic areas
- Serology two-step (EIA → Western blot) — interpret by timing ([[Serology in Clinical Microbiology]])
- [[PCR]] on EM biopsy early; CSF PCR limited sensitivity

## Treatment Notes (conceptual)
- Doxycycline, amoxicillin, ceftriaxone by stage/system involvement
- Prophylaxis after tick bite — selective per guidelines
