# Cytomegalovirus

## One-Sentence Definition

Cytomegalovirus (CMV) is a beta-herpesvirus with lifelong latency that causes significant disease in immunocompromised hosts (transplant, HIV) and congenital infection when acquired in pregnancy.

## Taxonomy & Morphology
- Human herpesvirus 5; dsDNA; envelope; infects many cell types
- Large genome; slow replication compared to alpha-herpesviruses

## Virulence Highlights
- Latency in myeloid lineage cells
- Immunoevasion — modulates MHC, innate sensing
- Congenital CMV — leading infectious cause of birth defects (hearing loss, neuro)

## Clinical Syndromes
- Immunocompetent: often mononucleosis-like or asymptomatic
- Transplant: pneumonitis, retinitis, colitis, hepatitis
- [[HIV]] with low CD4: retinitis, GI disease
- Congenital: microcephaly, hearing loss, chorioretinitis

## Diagnosis
- Quantitative **CMV DNA [[PCR]]** (viral load) — transplant monitoring
- pp65 antigenemia (historical)
- Shell vial / culture (slower)
- Congenital: urine/saliva PCR within 3 weeks of life

## Treatment / Prevention
- Antivirals (ganciclovir/valganciclovir, foscarnet, cidofovir) — toxicity monitoring
- Preemptive vs prophylactic strategies in transplant
- No routine vaccine in widespread use (research ongoing)
