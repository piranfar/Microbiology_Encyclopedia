# Influenza Virus

## One-Sentence Definition

Influenza viruses (Orthomyxoviridae; types A/B/C) are enveloped, segmented negative-sense RNA viruses that cause seasonal epidemics and occasional pandemics through antigenic drift and — for influenza A — antigenic shift.

## Taxonomy & Structure
- Types: A (pandemic potential; HA/NA subtypes), B (human), C (milder), D (mainly cattle)
- Genome: 8 segments (A/B) of (−)ssRNA; RNA-dependent RNA polymerase
- Envelope glycoproteins: **hemagglutinin (HA)** — entry; **neuraminidase (NA)** — release
- Matrix (M1), M2 ion channel (A), NP, NS1 (interferon antagonism)

## Replication & Pathogenesis Highlights
- Respiratory epithelial tropism; cytokine storm in severe disease
- **Antigenic drift** — gradual HA/NA mutations → seasonal vaccine updates ([[Antigenic Variation]])
- **Antigenic shift** — segment reassortment (often swine/avian interfaces) → pandemic strains
- Secondary bacterial pneumonia (*S. aureus*, pneumococcus) important complication

## Clinical Syndromes
- Abrupt fever, myalgia, cough, headache; otitis/pneumonia in children/elderly
- Complications: myocarditis, encephalitis (rare), exacerbation of COPD/CHF
- Avian zoonoses (H5N1, H7N9…) — severe pneumonia, high mortality when they infect humans

## Diagnosis
- Rapid antigen (lower sensitivity) · [[RT-PCR]] / [[qPCR]] preferred · syndromic respiratory panels ([[Syndromic Molecular Panels]])
- Specimen: NP swab / aspirate early; [[RNA Extraction]] critical
- Subtyping and clade assignment via sequencing for surveillance ([[Viral Genomics and Surveillance]])

## Treatment & Prevention (conceptual)
- Neuraminidase inhibitors (oseltamivir, zanamivir…); baloxavir (cap-dependent endonuclease inhibitor) — start early
- Resistance mutations (e.g., H275Y in NA) monitored genotypically
- Annual inactivated / live attenuated / recombinant vaccines; composition updated by WHO ([[Vaccination]])
- Infection control: droplet precautions; antivirals for prophylaxis in outbreaks

## Genomics & Computational Notes
- HA/NA phylogenies for vaccine strain selection ([[Phylodynamics]])
- Reassortment detection across segments
- Antiviral resistance SNP calling from clinical sequences
