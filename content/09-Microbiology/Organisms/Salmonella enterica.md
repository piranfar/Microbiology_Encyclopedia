# Salmonella enterica

## One-Sentence Definition

*Salmonella enterica* is a Gram-negative Enterobacterales pathogen encompassing typhoidal serovars (enteric fever) and thousands of non-typhoidal serovars causing gastroenteritis and invasive disease, structured by virulence [[Genomic Islands]] (SPI).

## Taxonomy & Morphology
- Gram-negative bacillus; usually motile; lactose non-fermenter
- Serotyping by O (LPS) and H (flagellar) antigens — increasingly predicted from WGS
- Major pathovars: Typhi/Paratyphi vs non-typhoidal *Salmonella* (NTS: Typhimurium, Enteritidis, …)

## Virulence Highlights
- **SPI-1** — invasion of intestinal epithelium
- **SPI-2** — intracellular survival in macrophages
- Additional SPIs, prophages, and plasmids modify host range and resistance
- Vi capsule in Typhi

## Clinical Syndromes
- NTS: foodborne gastroenteritis; bacteremia in immunocompromised / infants
- Typhoid/paratyphoid: prolonged fever, systemic illness
- Focal infections (osteomyelitis, endovascular) possible

## Diagnosis
- Stool/blood culture; selective media
- Serotyping or WGS-based serotype prediction
- AST for invasive/typhoidal cases — resistance rising globally

## Treatment Notes (conceptual)
- Uncomplicated NTS gastroenteritis: often supportive (antibiotics can prolong carriage)
- Invasive / typhoidal: directed therapy; watch MDR/XDR typhoid epidemiology

## AMR
- MDR plasmids historically (ACSSuT patterns); extensively drug-resistant Typhi clones with plasmid + chromosomal resistance
- Fluoroquinolone resistance via gyrase/topoisomerase mutations
- Links to [[Integrons]] and [[Plasmid]] ecology in agriculture/One Health ([[One Health]])

## Genomics & Computational Notes
- Classic model for [[Genomic Islands]] and [[Prophage Detection and Annotation]]
- Outbreak typing: cgMLST / SNP pipelines in PulseNet-style networks
- Recombination moderate; still use careful phylogenomics ([[Phylogenomics and Outbreak Typing]])
