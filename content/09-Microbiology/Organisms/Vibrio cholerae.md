# Vibrio cholerae

## One-Sentence Definition

*Vibrio cholerae* is a curved, oxidase-positive Gram-negative bacillus of brackish/marine environments; toxigenic O1/O139 strains cause epidemic cholera via cholera toxin–driven secretory diarrhea.

## Taxonomy & Morphology
- Vibrionaceae; comma-shaped rods; polar flagellum; grows on TCBS agar
- Serogroups: **O1** and **O139** — epidemic cholera; non-O1/non-O139 — sporadic gastroenteritis/wound/sepsis
- Biotypes of O1: classical vs El Tor (current pandemic biotype)

## Virulence Highlights
- **Cholera toxin (CT)** — AB₅ toxin; ADP-ribosylates Gs → ↑cAMP → massive Cl⁻/water secretion
- *ctx* genes on CTXφ prophage ([[Prophage Detection and Annotation]], [[Transduction]])
- **TCP** (toxin-coregulated pilus) — colonization; also phage receptor
- Regulated by ToxR/ToxT network ([[Bacterial Operons and Sigma Factors]])

## Clinical Syndromes
- Profuse watery “rice-water” stool → hypovolemic shock if untreated
- Mild/asymptomatic infections common; severe disease in outbreaks and fragile water systems
- Related species: *V. parahaemolyticus*, *V. vulnificus* (seafood/wound — different syndromes)

## Diagnosis
- Clinical recognition in outbreaks + dark-field / culture on TCBS
- Rapid antigen / [[PCR]] in reference and outbreak settings
- Serogrouping for O1/O139; toxigenicity assays when needed

## Treatment Notes (conceptual)
- **Rehydration is the therapy** (ORS / IV) — antibiotics shorten shedding and severity in severe cases
- Doxycycline, azithromycin, or ciprofloxacin per local resistance — stewardship during epidemics
- Zinc in children; vaccines in outbreak/travel contexts ([[Vaccination]])

## AMR
- Tetracycline/fluoroquinolone/macrolide resistance reported in circulating strains
- Plasmid and chromosomal contributions; monitor during outbreak response
- [[One Health]] / water sanitation primary control — drugs alone do not stop epidemics

## Genomics & Computational Notes
- Seventh pandemic El Tor lineage tracked by [[Phylodynamics]] / SNP typing
- Horizontal acquisition of CTXφ and pathogenicity islands is classic teaching
- Environmental reservoirs and climate/water links for surveillance models
