# Streptococcus pneumoniae

## One-Sentence Definition

*Streptococcus pneumoniae* (pneumococcus) is a Gram-positive lancet-shaped diplococcus and leading cause of community pneumonia, meningitis, and otitis — virulence driven by a polysaccharide [[Capsule]].

## Taxonomy & Morphology
- α-hemolytic; optochin-sensitive; bile-soluble (classic)
- Encapsulated; many serotypes
- [[Gram Stain]]: lancet-shaped diplococci ± neutrophils

## Virulence Highlights
- Polysaccharide capsule (anti-phagocytic) — vaccine target
- Pneumolysin, adhesins
- Natural competence → [[Transformation]] and mosaic PBPs

## Clinical Syndromes
- Lobar pneumonia, bacteremia, meningitis
- Otitis media, sinusitis

## Diagnosis
- Sputum/blood/CSF Gram + culture
- Urine pneumococcal antigen (syndrome-dependent)
- Multiplex PCR on CSF/respiratory panels

## Treatment Notes (conceptual)
- Guided by local resistance and syndrome (meningitis breakpoints differ)
- Empiric regimens must cover possible penicillin/ceftriaxone non-susceptibility until AST

## AMR
- Penicillin / β-lactam non-susceptibility via altered PBPs (often transformational mosaics)
- Macrolide and MDR patterns vary by serotype/region

## Prevention
- Pneumococcal conjugate / polysaccharide vaccines (serotype coverage matters)
