# Corynebacterium diphtheriae

## One-Sentence Definition

*Corynebacterium diphtheriae* is a Gram-positive rod whose toxigenic strains produce diphtheria toxin (via lysogenic phage), causing pseudomembranous pharyngitis and systemic toxicity — prevented by toxoid vaccination.

## Taxonomy & Morphology
- Club-shaped Gram-positive bacillus; metachromatic granules
- Non-spore-forming; tellurite selective media (historical ID)

## Virulence Highlights
- **Diphtheria toxin** — ADP-ribosylates EF-2 → protein synthesis arrest
- Toxin gene carried by β-phage ([[Transduction]])
- Non-toxigenic strains cause milder colonization/infection

## Clinical Syndromes
- Respiratory diphtheria — pseudomembrane, airway obstruction
- Cutaneous diphtheria (tropical/travel)
- Systemic toxin: myocarditis, neuropathy

## Diagnosis
- Culture from throat/lesion; **toxin PCR/Elek test** for toxigenicity
- Do not delay antitoxin on culture if clinical suspicion high

## Treatment / Prevention
- Diphtheria antitoxin + antibiotics (penicillin/macrolide)
- **DTaP/Tdap** toxoid vaccine ([[Vaccination]])
- Close contact monitoring and prophylaxis per public health
