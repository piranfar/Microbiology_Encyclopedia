# Rickettsia rickettsii

## One-Sentence Definition

*Rickettsia rickettsii* is an obligate intracellular alphaproteobacterium transmitted by tick bite, causing Rocky Mountain spotted fever — a vasculitis-prone illness requiring prompt doxycycline.

## Taxonomy & Morphology
- Rickettsiales; small coccobacillus; grows in host cells only
- Related: *R. typhi* (murine typhus), *Orientia tsutsugamushi* (scrub typhus)

## Virulence Highlights
- Endothelial infection → vasculitis, edema, hemorrhage
- Spread via tick saliva during feeding

## Clinical Syndromes
- Fever, headache, rash (often starts peripherally; can be absent early)
- Severe: DIC, encephalitis, multiorgan failure
- History of tick exposure or travel — critical

## Diagnosis
- Clinical suspicion + serology (paired IgG) — acute serology often negative early
- [[PCR]] on skin biopsy/blood in reference labs
- Do **not** withhold doxycycline waiting for labs

## Treatment Notes (conceptual)
- Doxycycline first line (including children — benefit outweighs tooth staining risk in short course per guidelines)
- Chloramphenicol alternative where doxycycline unavailable
