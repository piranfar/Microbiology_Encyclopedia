# Pseudomonas aeruginosa

## One-Sentence Definition

*Pseudomonas aeruginosa* is an oxidase-positive Gram-negative non-fermenter that thrives in moist environments and causes opportunistic infections with frequent MDR phenotypes and biofilm-associated disease.

## Taxonomy & Morphology
- Gram-negative rod; non-lactose fermenter
- Grape-like odor; blue-green pigments (pyocyanin/pyoverdine) classic
- [[Gram Stain]]: pink rods

## Virulence Highlights
- [[Biofilm]] (CF airways, devices)
- Exotoxins (ExoS/ExoT/ExoU via T3SS), elastases, pigments
- Intrinsic impermeability + efflux

## Clinical Syndromes
- VAP, burn wound infection, otitis externa (malignant in diabetics)
- Bacteremia in neutropenic/hospitalized patients
- Chronic CF lung infection

## Diagnosis
- Culture on standard media; oxidase-positive ID
- AST mandatory for therapy
- Molecular panels may include *Pseudomonas* targets

## Treatment Notes (conceptual)
- Antipseudomonal β-lactams / fluoroquinolones / aminoglycosides per AST and syndrome
- Combination therapy sometimes used in severe disease — practice varies
- Biofilm/device infections need source control

## AMR
- Intrinsic resistance high baseline
- Acquired: carbapenemases, upregulated efflux, porin (OprD) loss
- Difficult-to-treat resistance (DTR) category in modern stewardship language
