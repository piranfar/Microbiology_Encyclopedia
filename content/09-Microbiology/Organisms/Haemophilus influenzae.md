# Haemophilus influenzae

## One-Sentence Definition

*Haemophilus influenzae* is a fastidious Gram-negative coccobacillus causing otitis, sinusitis, pneumonia, and — historically via type b capsule — pediatric meningitis; conjugate vaccination transformed invasive Hib epidemiology.

## Taxonomy & Morphology
- Gram-negative coccobacillus; requires X (hemin) and/or V (NAD) factors
- Grows on chocolate agar; satellite growth around *S. aureus* on blood agar
- Typeable (capsule a–f, Hib = type b) vs nontypeable (NTHi, unencapsulated)

## Virulence Highlights
- Hib [[Capsule]] (PRP) — major invasive disease driver pre-vaccine
- NTHi: adhesins, biofilm in middle ear / airways ([[Biofilm]])
- IgA protease; LOS endotoxin

## Clinical Syndromes
- Hib (unvaccinated): meningitis, epiglottitis, bacteremia, septic arthritis
- NTHi: otitis media, sinusitis, COPD exacerbations, community pneumonia
- Non-b encapsulated types (a, e, f) cause residual invasive disease

## Diagnosis
- Culture on chocolate; MALDI; CSF/blood PCR panels
- Capsule typing for invasive isolates (surveillance)

## Treatment Notes (conceptual)
- Empiric meningitis regimens cover Hib; definitive therapy by AST
- β-lactamase-producing strains common — ampicillin resistance historically important
- Vaccination prevents most Hib disease ([[Vaccination]])

## AMR
- TEM β-lactamases → ampicillin resistance
- BLNAR (β-lactamase-negative ampicillin-resistant) via PBP changes
- Watch β-lactamase + altered PBP combinations

## Genomics & Computational Notes
- Capsule loci and BLNAR mutations detectable by WGS
- NTHi population structure highly diverse — recombination ([[Recombination in Bacterial Phylogenies]])
