# Neisseria gonorrhoeae

## One-Sentence Definition

*Neisseria gonorrhoeae* (gonococcus) is a Gram-negative diplococcus causing urethritis, cervicitis, pelvic inflammatory disease, and disseminated infection — now a critical AMR threat with shrinking therapeutic options.

## Taxonomy & Morphology
- Gram-negative kidney-bean diplococci, often intracellular on smear
- Fastidious; CO₂-loving; Thayer-Martin / modern selective media
- Closely related to [[Neisseria meningitidis]] but niche = mucosa of urogenital tract, rectum, pharynx

## Virulence Highlights
- Type IV pili — attachment; phase/antigenic variation ([[Antigenic Variation]])
- Opa proteins; LOS; IgA protease
- Natural competence → rampant recombination ([[Bacterial Competence Systems]])

## Clinical Syndromes
- Urethritis, cervicitis; often asymptomatic in women
- PID, infertility, ectopic pregnancy risk
- Disseminated gonococcal infection (DGI): arthritis-dermatitis
- Ophthalmia neonatorum; pharyngeal/rectal infection

## Diagnosis
- NAAT preferred for genital sites; culture still needed for AST
- Gram stain of urethral discharge (symptomatic men) — high PPV
- Extragenital NAAT where indicated

## Treatment Notes (conceptual)
- Follow current CDC/WHO guidelines — regimens change with resistance
- Dual therapy historically; ceftriaxone backbone evolving by dose/region
- Test of cure for pharyngeal infection in many protocols

## AMR
- **Urgent threat:** resistance to penicillins, tetracyclines, fluoroquinolones; rising cephalosporin/azithromycin MIC
- Mosaic *penA* alleles (cephalosporin); gyrase/parC; efflux (Mtr); rRNA mutations
- Culture-based AST essential for treatment failures
