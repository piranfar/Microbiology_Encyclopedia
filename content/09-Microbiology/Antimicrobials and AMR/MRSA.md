# MRSA

## One-Sentence Definition

MRSA (methicillin-resistant *Staphylococcus aureus*) denotes *S. aureus* isolates resistant to nearly all β-lactams via acquired PBP2a (encoded by *mecA* or *mecC*), typically carried on the SCCmec genomic island.

## Simple Explanation

The staph rewires its cell-wall-building enzyme so methicillin/oxacillin (and most related β-lactams) no longer bind — a genetic cassette makes ordinary penicillins useless.

## Detailed Scientific Explanation

| Element | Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| *mecA* / *mecC* | Encodes PBP2a / PBP2c with low β-lactam affinity |
| SCCmec | [[Genomic Islands\|Resistance island]] integrating at *orfX* |
| *mecR1* / *mecI* | Regulation of *mecA* induction |
| HA-MRSA vs CA-MRSA | Hospital clones (often MDR) vs community (often PVL+, narrower resistance) |

Phenotypic detection: cefoxitin screen, oxacillin MIC, PBP2a immunoassays; confirm with *mec* PCR when discrepant. Borderline oxacillin-resistant *S. aureus* (BORSA) lacks *mecA* — different mechanism.

## Mechanism

SCCmec acquisition → PBP2a takes over transpeptidation in presence of β-lactams → cell wall synthesis continues → methicillin/oxacillin/nafcillin and most cephalosporins fail (ceftaroline/ceftobiprole are anti-MRSA cephalosporin exceptions).

## Clinical Importance

- Empiric therapy for severe staphylococcal disease must consider local MRSA rates.
- Infection control: contact precautions, decolonization protocols in outbreaks.
- Distinguishes MSSA (anti-staph β-lactams preferred) from MRSA.

## Research Importance

- SCCmec typing, CC5/CC8/CC30 genomics, vaccine and anti-virulence research.
- Model for [[Genomic Islands]] and [[Horizontal Gene Transfer]] in Gram-positives.

## Diagnostic Relevance

- Rapid *mecA*/PBP2a assays guide therapy hours before AST finalization.
- WGS for outbreak typing of MRSA clones ([[Population Structure and Clustering]]).

## AMR Relevance

**Flagship Gram-positive AMR.** Often co-resists macrolides, fluoroquinolones, sometimes vancomycin-intermediate (VISA) phenotypes via cell-wall thickening — distinct from *mec*.

## Learning Aids

### Clinical Example
> [!example]
> **Case:** Purulent cellulitis; wound grows *S. aureus*; cefoxitin screen resistant; *mecA* PCR positive.
> **Question:** Is oxacillin an option?
> **Answer:** No — MRSA; use anti-MRSA agents (vancomycin, daptomycin, linezolid, TMP-SMX, doxycycline, ceftaroline — site-dependent).
