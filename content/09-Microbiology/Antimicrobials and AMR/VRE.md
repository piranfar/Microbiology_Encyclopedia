# VRE

## One-Sentence Definition

VRE (vancomycin-resistant enterococci) are *Enterococcus* isolates — most often *E. faecium* — with acquired *van* gene clusters that remodel peptidoglycan termini so vancomycin (and often teicoplanin) can no longer bind.

## Simple Explanation

The enterococcus changes the “hook” on its cell wall that vancomycin grabs, so the drug slips off — usually after picking up a mobile resistance cassette.

## Detailed Scientific Explanation

| Genotype | Ligand change | Typical vancomycin | Teicoplanin |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VanA** | D-Ala-D-Lac | High-level R | R |
| **VanB** | D-Ala-D-Lac | Variable R | often S |
| VanC | Intrinsic (*E. gallinarum/casseliflavus*) | Low-level | S |
| VanD/E/G… | Less common | Variable | Variable |

- *vanA* often on Tn1546-like elements / plasmids ([[Transposons and Insertion Sequences]], [[Plasmid]])
- Inducible via [[Two-Component Regulatory Systems]] (VanRS) — exposure raises expression
- Host: predominantly [[Enterococcus faecium]] hospital clade; *E. faecalis* VRE less common but important

## Mechanism

Van enzymes synthesize D-Ala-D-Lac (or D-Ser) ending precursors → vancomycin cannot bind depsipeptide with high affinity → cell wall synthesis continues.

## Clinical Importance

- ICU/transplant bacteremia, device infection, endocarditis treatment crises
- Contact precautions; rectal screening in outbreaks ([[Infection Prevention and Control]])
- Therapy: linezolid, daptomycin (optimize), sometimes tigecycline/combination — stewardship mandatory ([[Antimicrobial Stewardship]])
- Risk of *vanA* transfer toward *S. aureus* (VRSA — rare but watched)

## Research Importance

- Plasmid epidemiology of VRE; One Health links limited vs Gram-negatives but exist
- WGS/cgMLST for hospital clusters ([[Population Structure and Clustering]])

## Diagnostic Relevance

- Vancomycin MIC + confirm species (avoid calling intrinsic VanC organisms “VRE” in the same IPC sense)
- *vanA*/*vanB* [[PCR]] on isolates or screening swabs
- [[Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing]] remains definitive for therapy

## AMR Relevance

**Flagship Gram-positive nosocomial AMR** alongside [[MRSA]]; core [[ESKAPE Pathogens]] member.

## Learning Aids

### Clinical Example
> [!example]
> **Case:** Bone marrow transplant patient; blood culture *E. faecium*; vancomycin MIC >256; *vanA* PCR positive.
> **Question:** Is teicoplanin likely active?
> **Answer:** Usually no for VanA — choose an anti-VRE agent and source control; isolate for IPC.
