# Antimicrobial Stewardship

## One-Sentence Definition

Antimicrobial stewardship is the coordinated effort to give the right antimicrobial, at the right dose, by the right route, for the right duration — and to avoid giving one at all when it is not needed.

## Simple Explanation

Not "use fewer antibiotics" but "use them well." Sometimes that means broader and faster; usually it means narrower and shorter.

## Detailed Scientific Explanation

**The five Ds:** right Diagnosis, right Drug, right Dose, right Duration, De-escalation.

**Core interventions**
- Prospective audit with feedback; formulary restriction and pre-authorization
- **De-escalation** once culture and susceptibility return
- **Duration limits** — evidence increasingly supports shorter courses (many pneumonias, UTIs, intra-abdominal infections)
- IV-to-oral switch when absorption and clinical state allow
- Allergy de-labelling (most reported penicillin allergies are not true allergies)
- Diagnostic stewardship — do not culture what you will not treat; avoid treating asymptomatic bacteriuria
- Rapid diagnostics coupled to intervention ([[AI Diagnostics in Microbiology]])

**Measurement:** DDD or days of therapy per 1000 patient-days, appropriateness audits, *C. difficile* rates, resistance trends, and clinical outcomes.

**Why it must not be naive:** delayed effective therapy in severe [[Sepsis]] costs lives. Stewardship balances two harms rather than minimizing one.

## Mechanism
Reducing unnecessary selection pressure lowers the fitness advantage of resistant strains and reduces collateral damage to the [[Human Microbiome]].

## Clinical Importance
- Fewer adverse drug events, less *Clostridioides difficile* infection, lower cost, preserved options

## Research Importance
- One of few AI application areas with real prospective evaluation potential — see [[AI in Antimicrobial Stewardship]]

## Diagnostic Relevance
- Depends entirely on timely, interpretable laboratory reporting; selective reporting of susceptibilities is a stewardship tool

## AMR Relevance
Together with [[Infection Prevention and Control]] and [[Vaccination]], it is one of the three practical levers against [[Antimicrobial Resistance]].
