# ESKAPE Pathogens

## One-Sentence Definition

ESKAPE is an acronym for six bacterial species that most often "escape" the activity of standard antibiotics and dominate serious hospital-acquired infections.

## Simple Explanation

Six organisms that account for a large share of untreatable hospital infections — worth knowing as a group.

## Detailed Scientific Explanation

| Letter | Organism | Signature resistance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **E** | *Enterococcus faecium* | Vancomycin resistance (VRE, *vanA*/*vanB*) |
| **S** | *[[Staphylococcus aureus]]* | Methicillin resistance (MRSA, *mecA* → PBP2a) |
| **K** | *[[Klebsiella pneumoniae]]* | ESBL, carbapenemases (KPC, NDM, OXA-48) |
| **A** | *[[Acinetobacter baumannii]]* | Carbapenem resistance (OXA-23), pan-drug resistance |
| **P** | *[[Pseudomonas aeruginosa]]* | Efflux + porin loss + AmpC; intrinsic multidrug resistance |
| **E** | *Enterobacter* species | Inducible AmpC, carbapenemases |

Some versions extend the list to **ESKAPEE** by adding *[[Escherichia coli]]*.

Shared features: hospital and device association ([[Device-Associated Infections]]), [[Biofilm]] formation, ready acquisition of [[Plasmid]]-borne resistance, and environmental persistence.

The WHO **bacterial priority pathogens list** covers similar ground with a formal prioritization framework and drives research funding.

## Clinical Importance
- Concentrated in ICUs, transplant, oncology, and burn units
- Frequently the reason a patient needs last-line agents (colistin, ceftazidime-avibactam, cefiderocol)

## Research Importance
- The target set for [[AI for Antibiotic Discovery]] and new-agent development

## Diagnostic Relevance
- Rapid identification and resistance detection changes therapy within hours

## AMR Relevance
This group defines the practical urgency of [[Antimicrobial Resistance]].

## Public Health
- Surveillance priority; [[Infection Prevention and Control]] targets; [[One Health]] reservoirs for some species
