# Complement System

## One-Sentence Definition

The complement system is a cascade of plasma proteins that opsonizes pathogens, recruits inflammation, and lyses susceptible cells — bridging [[Innate Immunity]] and [[Antibodies and Immunoglobulin Classes]]-mediated defense.

## Simple Explanation

A cascade of blood proteins that punch holes in bacteria, coat them for eating, and amplify inflammation when antibodies or microbes trigger it.

## Detailed Scientific Explanation

**Three activation pathways**
| Pathway | Trigger | Classic micro example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Classical** | IgM/IgG + antigen | Encapsulated bacteria after antibody binding |
| **Alternative** | Microbial surfaces directly | Neisseria (when unprotected) |
| **Lectin** | Mannose on microbes | Gram-negative surfaces |

**Effector functions**
- **C3b opsonization** — enhanced phagocytosis
- **C5a** — chemotaxis, anaphylatoxin
- **MAC (C5b-9)** — lysis of susceptible cells (especially Gram-negative, some enveloped viruses)

**Clinical deficiencies**
- **C5–C9 / terminal pathway** → recurrent **Neisseria** bacteremia/meningitis ([[Neisseria meningitidis]], [[Neisseria gonorrhoeae]])
- **C3 deficiency** → pyogenic infections broadly
- **Properdin (alternative)** → Neisseria susceptibility

**Microbial evasion**
- [[Capsule]] (pneumococcus, meningococcus)
- Surface proteins that bind factor H (some Gram-negatives)
- Intracellular location avoids lysis

## Mechanism
Proteolytic cascade amplifies cleavage of C3 → opsonins and lytic complex; tightly regulated to limit host damage.

## Clinical Importance
- Explains why asplenic and complement-deficient patients need meningococcal vaccination
- Complement consumption in severe sepsis (CH50 low — research/clinical context)

## Research Importance
- Vaccine design against encapsulated organisms targets antibody + complement synergy

## Diagnostic Relevance
- Complement levels in recurrent Neisseria workups (specialist)

## AMR Relevance
- None direct — immunologic, not pharmacologic
