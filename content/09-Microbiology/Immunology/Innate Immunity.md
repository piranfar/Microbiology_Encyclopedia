# Innate Immunity

## One-Sentence Definition

Innate immunity is the immediate, non-antigen-specific host defense — physical barriers, phagocytes, pattern recognition, inflammation, and [[Complement System]] — that contains microbes before [[Adaptive Immunity]] can respond.

## Simple Explanation

The body's first responders: skin, mucus, neutrophils, and alarm signals that react to anything that looks microbial, without needing prior exposure.

## Detailed Scientific Explanation

| Component | Role | Micro relevance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Barriers** | Skin, mucosa, cilia, low pH, lysozyme | Prevents colonization → invasion |
| **Pattern recognition** | TLRs, NLRs, C-type lectins | Detect LPS, peptidoglycan, flagellin, viral RNA |
| **Phagocytes** | Neutrophils, macrophages, dendritic cells | Engulf/kill; present antigen to T cells |
| **NK cells** | Kill infected/stressed cells | Viral control early |
| **Inflammation** | Cytokines, chemokines, vasodilation | Recruit cells; fever |
| **Complement** | Opsonization, lysis, inflammation | See [[Complement System]] |

**Why it matters in microbiology**
- [[Capsule]] and biofilm evade phagocytosis ([[Biofilm]])
- Endotoxin (LPS) drives sepsis cytokine storm
- Dendritic cells bridge innate → adaptive response
- Immunodeficiency of innate pathways → opportunistic infections

## Mechanism
PRRs bind PAMPs/DAMPs → NF-κB / interferon pathways → effector recruitment and antigen presentation.

## Clinical Importance
- Neutropenia → Gram-negative sepsis risk
- Complement deficiency → recurrent *Neisseria* infections
- TLR signaling defects → unusual pathogen susceptibility

## Research Importance
- Adjuvant design for [[Vaccination]]
- Host-response signatures in [[Microbial Transcriptomics]]

## Diagnostic Relevance
- Inflammatory markers (CRP, procalcitonin) support but do not replace culture/PCR
- Neutrophil dysfunction syndromes in recurrent infections

## AMR Relevance
- Innate clearance reduces bacterial burden → less antibiotic pressure in some settings
