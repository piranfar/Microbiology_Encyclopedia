# Microbiology

## Definition
Microbiology is the scientific study of microorganisms — organisms and infectious agents typically too small to be seen clearly with the unaided eye — including bacteria, archaea, viruses, fungi, and parasites (protozoa and helminths in medical contexts).

**Medical microbiology** focuses on microbes that cause or influence human disease, their diagnosis, treatment, and control.

## Key Characteristics
- Depends historically on microscopy ([[Antonie van Leeuwenhoek]], [[light microscope]]).
- Organized today by domain hubs: [[MOC - Bacteriology]], [[MOC - Virology]], [[MOC - Mycology]], [[MOC - Parasitology]].
- Causality framework: [[Germ Theory]] and [[Koch’s Postulates]].
