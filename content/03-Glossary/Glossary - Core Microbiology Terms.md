# Glossary - Core Microbiology Terms

One-line definitions. Where a full note exists, the term links to it.

**Index:** [[Glossary Index]] · **Hub:** [[MOC - Fundamentals of Microbiology]]

## Cell structure
- **Peptidoglycan** — mesh polymer of sugar chains cross-linked by peptides that gives the bacterial wall its strength; the β-lactam target. See [[Bacterial Cell Wall]].
- **[[Capsule]]** — outer polysaccharide layer resisting phagocytosis and drying; a major virulence and vaccine target.
- **Teichoic acid** — anionic polymer in Gram-positive walls; surface charge and immune interaction.
- **LPS (endotoxin)** — lipopolysaccharide of the Gram-negative outer membrane; lipid A triggers septic shock.
- **Porin** — outer-membrane channel allowing small hydrophilic molecules in; losing porins reduces antibiotic entry.
- **Periplasm** — compartment between inner and outer membranes of Gram-negatives; where β-lactamases work.
- **Pilus / fimbria** — surface appendage for adhesion; the sex pilus mediates [[Conjugation]].
- **Endospore** — dormant, highly resistant survival structure (*Bacillus*, *Clostridium*, [[Clostridioides difficile]]).
- **[[Biofilm]]** — surface-attached community in a self-produced matrix; tolerant to antibiotics and immunity.

## Growth and physiology
- **Generation time** — time to double; minutes for *E. coli*, ~18 h for [[Mycobacterium tuberculosis]].
- **[[Bacterial Growth Curve|Lag / log / stationary / death phases]]** — the four phases of batch culture.
- **Obligate aerobe / anaerobe / facultative / microaerophilic** — oxygen requirement classes.
- **Fastidious** — needs enriched media or special atmosphere to grow.
- **Fermentation** — ATP generation without an external electron acceptor; the basis of many identification tests.
- **Auxotroph** — cannot synthesize a required nutrient; must be supplied.

## Genetics
- **Genome** — total genetic content; bacterial chromosomes are typically single and circular.
- **[[Plasmid]]** — extrachromosomal replicon, often carrying resistance or virulence genes.
- **Operon** — genes transcribed from one promoter as a single mRNA. See [[Gene Expression]].
- **[[Horizontal Gene Transfer]]** — acquisition of DNA from a non-parent, by [[Transformation]], [[Transduction]], or [[Conjugation]].
- **Transposon / insertion sequence** — mobile DNA that relocates within or between replicons.
- **Integron** — gene-capture system that collects cassettes, frequently resistance cassettes.
- **Prophage** — bacteriophage genome integrated into the host chromosome.
- **[[Mutation and Selection|Mutation]]** — heritable sequence change; the raw material for adaptation.
- **Hypermutator** — strain with defective DNA repair and elevated mutation rate; common in chronic infection.
- **Competence** — physiological state permitting uptake of external DNA.

## Taxonomy and classification
- **[[Microbial Classification|Taxonomy]]** — the naming and ranking system for organisms.
- **Species (bacterial)** — pragmatic definition; ~95% ANI is the common genomic threshold. See [[Comparative Genomics]].
- **Strain** — an isolate descended from a single colony; the unit of epidemiology.
- **Serotype** — variant defined by surface antigen reactivity (e.g., pneumococcal capsule types).
- **Clone / clonal complex** — group of closely related isolates; see [[MLST and cgMLST]].
- **16S rRNA gene** — conserved gene with variable regions used for phylogeny and identification ([[Carl Woese]], [[16S Amplicon Analysis]]).

## Host interaction
- **[[Pathogen]]** — organism capable of causing disease.
- **Opportunist** — causes disease mainly when host defences or barriers are compromised.
- **Commensal / [[Normal Microbiota]]** — resident organism not normally causing disease.
- **Colonization vs infection** — presence without host response vs presence with tissue damage and response.
- **Virulence factor** — trait contributing to pathogenicity (adhesin, toxin, capsule, secretion system).
- **Exotoxin / endotoxin** — secreted protein toxin vs LPS released from the Gram-negative envelope.
- **Zoonosis** — infection transmissible from animals to humans. See [[One Health]].
