# Pathology

## Definition
Pathology is the medical science of the causes, mechanisms, structural/functional changes, and effects of disease — including injury produced by infection, toxins, and immune responses.

## Key Characteristics
- Bridges basic science and clinical diagnosis (morphology + mechanisms).
- In infectious disease: damage may come from the [[Pathogen]], its toxins, or the host immune response ([[MOC - Immunology]]).
- Related clinical framing: [[Infectious Disease]].
